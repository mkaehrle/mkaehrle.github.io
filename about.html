About

I study biodiversity data policy and governance.

I am a PhD student in the Information School at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, advised by Kristin Eschenfelder. I am interested in biodiversity data policy and governance. I am training primarily as a social scientist, and my work increasingly draws on computational methods for collecting, harmonizing, and analyzing data.

Before returning to graduate school, I worked as an archivist. I currently work as a data curator with the Environmental Data Initiative ↗.
